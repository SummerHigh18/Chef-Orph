The Recipe

Ingridients:

  • 1 Can Condensed Cream
  • Cheese
  • 1 cup Milk
  • Black Pepper
  • 4 cups cooked corkscrew-shaped Pasta

Directions:

  1. Mix soup, cheeses, milk and black pepper in 1 1/2-qt. casserole. Stir in pasta.
  2. Stir it until it completely blends.
  3. Bake at 400 degrees F. for 20 min. or until hot.
  4. Garnish it and enjoy your free trip to Italy!
