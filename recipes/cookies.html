The Recipe

Ingridients:

  • Butter
  • Eggs
  • Vanilla Extract
  • Baking Soda
  • Flour
  • Choco Chips
  • Nuts

Directions:

  1. Gather your ingredients, making sure your butter is softened.
  2. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Beat butter, white sugar, and brown sugar together in a large bowl with an electric mixer until smooth and creamy.
  3. Dissolve baking soda in hot water; add to batter with vanilla along with salt and mix until combined.
  4. Stir in flour, chocolate chips, and walnuts until a soft dough forms.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven until edges are lightly browned, about 10 minutes.
  6. Cool on the baking sheets briefly before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely.
